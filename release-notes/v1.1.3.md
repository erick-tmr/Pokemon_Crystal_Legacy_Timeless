* **The Bug Catching Contest is repeatable again.** You can now enter it as often
  as you like on a contest day (Tuesday, Thursday, or Saturday) instead of being
  locked out after a single run — the once-per-day limit had no clock to reset in
  this RTC-less build, so it effectively stayed closed forever after the first try.
* **Full-Bag contest prizes are no longer lost.** If you placed and won a prize
  while your Bag was full, the National Park gate officer holds it for you — and
  will now always hand it back when you return, instead of the prize becoming
  unreachable.
* **Title screen bumped to V1.1.3** to match this release.
