* **Fixed the Celebi cutscene replaying on existing saves.** On a save that had
  already finished the intro, flying into New Bark Town could re-trigger the
  Celebi scene and then re-activate the old "you can't leave without a Pokémon"
  block at the town exits — trapping you in town. The cutscene now plays only
  during a fresh playthrough, as it was meant to.
* **Saves caught by that bug now repair themselves.** If a save was already
  stranded, it is fixed automatically the next time you enter New Bark Town —
  no restart or save editing needed.
* **Title screen bumped to V1.1.1** to match this release.
